# קיץ בהרי הרוקי — Rocky Mountain Summer

A walkable 3D simulation inspired by Rocky Mountain National Park, Colorado, in summer. Runs in the browser on desktop and phone (three.js, no build step).

Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder). `park.html` is the same page without the document wrapper, used for the hosted artifact; run `./build.sh` after editing it to regenerate `index.html`.

**World:** 6×6 km procedural terrain plus a distant mountain ring, glacial gorges, a meandering river and seven creeks with rapids, ten lakes in different colors (Bear, Nymph with pond lilies, Dream, Emerald, Haiyaha, The Loch, Sky Pond, Chasm, Sprague, Lawn), ~130k trees (Engelmann spruce, subalpine fir, lodgepole, ponderosa, aspen, krummholz, willows), wind-blown grass and wildflowers, alpine tundra, snowy peaks, seven marked trails with routed wooden signs and bridges, and wildlife (elk, moose, mule deer, bighorn sheep, marmots, red-tailed hawks).

**Controls:** WASD + mouse (click to lock), Shift run, Space jump, F fly, M map, T time of day. On phones: left-thumb joystick, right-thumb look, buttons for run/jump/fly/map. The map lets you jump to any point or destination.
