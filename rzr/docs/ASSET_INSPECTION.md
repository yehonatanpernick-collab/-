# Stage 1 — Inspection of the supplied models

Three `.glb` files were supplied. All were parsed directly (glTF JSON + binary buffers),
rendered in a headless browser, and measured. The originals are kept **byte-identical**
(SHA-256 verified) in `rzr/assets/source/` and `rzr/assets/vehicles/`.

## Key finding: the files are not what the brief describes

The brief lists *two terrain models + one RZR*. The files actually contain
**one terrain model and two RZR models**. There is no "Mountain Road Terrain Environment".

## Terrain model — not usable as a mountain landscape

* 4 meshes, 126,143 triangles, one material, **one 256×128 JPEG grass texture**.
* Geometry is a **100 m × 100 m plane** (node scale 50 × 4 × 50).
* Measured vertical relief across all 248k vertices: **≈3 mm** (y ∈ [−0.00037, 0.00040] × 4).
  It is effectively a flat lawn with very faint noise — no mountains, no road, no slopes.
* No collision geometry, no road geometry (nothing to drive on except the flat plane).
* Combining it with another terrain is moot; its texture (256 × 128) is far too low-res for
  close-up driving.

**Decision:** it cannot serve as the terrain foundation. It is preserved untouched in
`assets/source/` and documented here; the playable world is generated by
`tools/bake_world.mjs` (designed geography + erosion), textured with scanned CC0 materials.
If you meant to send a different "Mountain Road Terrain Environment" file, send it and it
can be evaluated the same way (the bake can import an external heightmap as the macro shape).

## RZR model A (`rzr_xp1000_ccby.glb`) — chosen as the player vehicle

* 26 meshes, 43,951 triangles, 5 materials (clear-coat paint, decals, tyres, glows), 7 PNG textures.
* Real-world scale: wheelbase **2.286 m**, track **1.40 m**, tyre radius **0.375 m** (30″) —
  matches the real XP 1000 High Lifter.
* Forward axis **+Z**, left **+X**, ground at y = 0.
* **Proper rig nodes per wheel**: `bone_wheel_XX_suspension → _steer → _rotation → WheelXX`
  (tyre + rim), plus brake calipers. These drive the in-game wheel pivots.
* Defects found and handled at load time (file untouched):
  * `lights_position_front_and_back_glows` — its front half is exported **1.2 m in front of
    the car** (spikes in front of the bumper). Stray triangles beyond z = 1.6 m are dropped.
  * `skinned_mesh_carpaint/_details` (suspension A-arms and shocks) lost their skin on
    export. A vertex shader deforms them toward each wheel hub by the live suspension travel.
* Licence CC-BY-4.0 permits commercial use with attribution.

## RZR model B (`rzr_xp1000_highlifter_ccbyncsa.glb`) — optional alternative

* 28 meshes, 43,935 triangles, 9 materials (red paint, emissive lights, glass).
* Exported at **1/100 scale** and **flattened** (all nodes identity; geometry baked in model
  space). Wheels are found by mesh name and re-pivoted at their bounding-box centres.
* **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0: non-commercial only**, share-alike. It is selectable in Settings but is
  not the default. Do not ship it in a commercial product.

## How each is used

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| Terrain GLB | Preserved, not loaded (flat 100 m plane — see above) |
| RZR A | Default player vehicle, full wheel/steer/suspension animation |
| RZR B | Optional vehicle (Settings → Vehicle), non-commercial licence flagged in UI |
